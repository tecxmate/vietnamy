## Summary

Pre-launch pipeline as of 2026-05-29. **193 web PWA users, near-zero DAU, 1–5 MAU.** Most importantly: **6 B2B-shaped customers have already signed up inbound** without any sales effort — Taiwan's largest private bank (CTBC) and 5 NTU Vietnamese-language classes. This is the wedge.

## B2B — landed (inbound, pre-launch)

### CTBC Bank (中國信託商業銀行)
- **Who:** Taiwan's largest private bank by assets. Operations in Vietnam (HCMC, Hanoi).
- **Use case:** Vietnamese fluency for relationship managers, ops, compliance teams.
- **Signal strength:** strongest possible — inbound, no B2B sales page exists yet, no marketing.
- **Next step:** scope the pilot (target 10–30 seats), price the Team tier, document a 6-month evaluation plan. Convert before app launch so we can announce a flagship.

### NTU × 5 Vietnamese language classes
- **Who:** Five in-person Vietnamese courses at National Taiwan University.
- **Use case:** Supplementary digital tool for in-person curriculum.
- **Distribution lever:** every class delivers 30+ students into the consumer funnel.
- **Inside track:** co-founder Quỳnh Võ is VP of NTU Vietnamese Student Association (NTU VSA). Direct line to the relevant faculty.
- **Next step:** package an academic site license at a discount; use as a reference for NCCU, NTHU, NCKU outreach.

## B2B — top targets (outbound, Q3–Q4 2026)

Ordered by expected time-to-close. All warm via existing TW network or Vietnamy partner relationships.

1. **Taiwanese banks in Vietnam.** Cathay United Bank, E.SUN, Mega International Bank — same use case as CTBC, leverage CTBC reference.
2. **Foxconn (鴻海) Vietnam.** 20 plants in Bac Ninh, ~USD 4B invested. Massive seat count potential; long sales cycle. Target via TW HQ HR or via VN subsidiary L&D.
3. **TW electronics manufacturing cluster.** Pegatron, Lite-On, Wistron NeWeb, Quanta, Compal — similar profile, plant managers + line supervisors.
4. **FPT Vietnam (TW office) + Taiwanese IT services.** Existing partner relationship via TECXWORK pipeline; mutual VN-TW staffing needs.
5. **Taiwan universities expanding Vietnamese language programs.** NCCU, NTHU, NCKU — academic pricing.
6. **Taipei Economic and Cultural Office (TECO) in HCMC and Hanoi.** Government use case; longer sales cycle but high credibility.

## B2C — current and target

- **Today:** 193 web PWA users, near-zero DAU, 1–5 MAU. Honest framing: pre-launch, no marketing, no app version.
- **12-month forecast:** 1,100 B2C registered users post aggressive marketing + app launch (Capacitor) + content acquisition.
- **Beachhead segments:**
  - Vietnamese diaspora in TW + Heritage learners reconnecting with the language.
  - Mandarin-speaking learners (TW + CN expats in VN).
  - Tourists planning trips to Vietnam (long-tail, lowest ARPU).

## Sales motion

- **B2B is founder-led.** Niko owns first 10 enterprise contracts. Move to a dedicated salesperson only after the playbook is documented and CAC converges.
- **NTU VSA distribution.** Quỳnh runs content + community; targets diaspora and academic audiences as primary B2C acquisition channels.
- **Tecxmate VN side.** HCMC team supports local sales conversations with TW companies' Vietnam subsidiaries.

## Open questions

- **CTBC contract size and timing.** Is the right first contract 10 seats (small pilot, low risk) or 50+ seats (proves Growth-tier pricing)?
- **Academic license model.** Per-student per-semester, per-class flat fee, or institution-wide?
- **Foxconn entry point.** TW HQ HR vs. VN plant manager vs. corporate L&D — different buyers, different ASPs.
- **B2C activation rate.** Current MAU is near-zero on the PWA. How much of that is product-fit vs. distribution? Capacitor + push notifications + paid acquisition all in the launch plan, but we should pick **one** ICP and validate before scaling.
