## Summary

Taiwan is the #4 foreign investor in Vietnam — 3,457 active projects, USD 42.37B accumulated capital (Apr 2026). Every one of those companies has a Vietnamese-language gap: Mandarin-speaking staff who need to talk to Vietnamese workers, sign Vietnamese contracts, and negotiate with Vietnamese vendors. Vietnamy's B2B beachhead is built around this corridor.

## Why this market is the wedge

- **Real money is flowing.** TW FDI into VN reached USD 2.8B in 2024 alone; Foxconn added USD 320M in adjusted capital in Quang Chau Industrial Park in 2025; Foxconn HQ in Hanoi opened April 2026 with 20 plants in Bac Ninh (~USD 4B invested).
- **The entire TW electronics ecosystem is there.** Foxconn, Pegatron, Lite-On, Panjit, Quanta, Wistron NeWeb, Accton, Compal — building clusters in Bắc Giang and Hải Phòng.
- **Today the only B2B option is interpreters.** ~USD 1,500/month per interpreter. No scalable team-seat SaaS for Vietnamese language exists. Duolingo for Business doesn't even offer Vietnamese.
- **CTBC Bank validated the demand.** Taiwan's largest private bank (中國信託商業銀行) came to Vietnamy inbound with no B2B sales effort. Confirms enterprises are actively shopping for this.

## Target enterprise segments

Ordered by likely time-to-close:

1. **Taiwanese banks operating in Vietnam.** CTBC, Cathay, E.SUN, Mega International — Mandarin-fluent staff need to navigate Vietnamese banking law, compliance, and customer-facing roles.
2. **Taiwanese manufacturers in northern VN.** Foxconn, Pegatron, Lite-On, Compal — line supervisors and ops managers managing Vietnamese factory floors.
3. **University Vietnamese language programs in Taiwan.** NTU (5 classes confirmed), NCCU, NTHU, NCKU — supplementary tool for in-person courses.
4. **Government / consultancy.** Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Hanoi/HCMC, MOEA New Southbound Policy programs, KPMG/PwC VN desks.

## Distribution channels

- **NTU VSA (Vietnamese Student Association).** Co-founder Quỳnh Võ is VP. Direct line to NTU Vietnamese-language faculty and student-org leadership.
- **Tecxmate Vietnam side.** HCMC R&D team has local relationships with VN-side counterparts of TW firms.
- **Diaspora communities in TW.** ~286K Vietnamese migrant workers + 40K students — both indirect channels to B2C and indirect references for B2B.

## Open questions

- Do we sell direct to HQ in Taipei, or to the VN subsidiary? (Bank HQ vs. factory floor are different buyers.)
- Per-seat vs. per-site pricing for multi-location manufacturers like Foxconn — single global contract or per-plant?
- Compliance: do we need a TW legal entity to invoice Taiwan enterprises with GUI (發票)? See `taiwan-legal-operational-framework` thinking in [tecxwork docs] for the sibling-LLC playbook.
- Whether to offer custom corporate-vocab onboarding as a paid add-on or include it in Enterprise tier.
