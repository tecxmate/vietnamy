What this prototype shows

A narrated explainer that turns your reading page into a slide presentation synced to audio.

  • Top: annotated-photo slides that auto-advance as the narration reaches them.
  • Bottom: the transcript with the current sentence highlighted and words sweeping one-by-one (the v1 syllable-estimate approach — no backend change).
  • Tap any word → meaning popup (reuses your TappableVietnamese + WordPopup).
  • Save a phrase to review (SRS) · Explain opens a usage note.

Press ▶ to start the narration. Tap words while it plays. Drag the seek bar, change speed, or jump lines.

Slides here are placeholders — in the app they're real annotated photos of airports, money, and the Grab app.
