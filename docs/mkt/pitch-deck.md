## Slide 3 — The Problem

### Title
Learning Vietnamese is broken — *especially for Mandarin speakers.*

### Subtitle (繁中)
學越南語的困境，對華語族群尤其嚴重

### Three problem cards

**Card 1 — Tone Blindness · 聲調盲區**
- Big stat: **0** (apps that grade Vietnamese tones)
- Headline: Apps that grade Vietnamese tones
- Body: Duolingo, Ling, Pimsleur — none can hear a tone error. Vietnamese has 6 tones. Get one wrong and "mother" becomes "horse". Every major app uses text-match grading.

**Card 2 — The Hán-Việt Gap · 漢越知識斷層**
- Big stat: **60%** (of Vietnamese is Sino-derived)
- Headline: of Vietnamese is Sino-derived — wasted
- Body: Mandarin & Cantonese speakers already half-know Vietnamese through shared Chinese-character roots (phát triển = 發展). No app teaches this. The biggest learner advantage in Asia is invisible.

**Card 3 — Enterprise Vacuum · 企業端真空**
- Big stat: **$1.5K/mo** (per interpreter)
- Headline: Per interpreter — the only B2B option
- Body: TW companies in VN have no scalable language-training option. They hire interpreters, run ad-hoc Zoom classes, or send staff to in-person Vietnamese schools. No team-seat SaaS exists.

### Bottom band
> Vietnamese is the most important language nobody can learn properly. 亞洲最被低估的語言：越南語。

### Talk track (~60s)
"Three problems compound. One: zero apps grade Vietnamese tones. Vietnamese has six tones — má means mother, mã means horse — and every major app scores both the same. Two: 60% of Vietnamese vocabulary comes from Chinese. Mandarin speakers already half-know the language, but no product surfaces this. Three: there is no team-seat SaaS for enterprises. They hire interpreters at $1,500 a month. Vietnamy fixes all three."

### Data / sources
- 60% Sino-Vietnamese vocab: Wikipedia, Sino-Vietnamese vocabulary
- 6 Vietnamese tones: standard linguistic fact
